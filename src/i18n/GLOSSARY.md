# Hindi glossary and voice

Hindi is the default language. Write natural, warm, respectful Hindi, the way a
cultured family in Lucknow or Varanasi would speak — not machine-literal, not
over-Sanskritised. Always address the reader as **आप**. Prefer everyday loanwords
people actually use (बुकिंग, OTP, ईमेल, ऑनलाइन) over invented coinages.

## Rules
- Write whole sentences with `{placeholders}`; never build a sentence by joining
  translated fragments (Hindi is verb-final, word order differs from English).
- Plurals: give `key_one` / `key_other` and call `t.plural('key', n)`.
- Buttons are short imperatives: "बुक करें", "सहेजें", "रद्द करें".
- Errors say what happened and what to do; they never apologise vaguely.
- Proper names of pujas, deities and places are written in Devanagari in Hindi.
- Numbers stay in Latin digits (Intl `hi-IN` does this by default).
